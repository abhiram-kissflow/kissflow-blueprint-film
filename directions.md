# Three directions — Kissflow AI Builder: the SDLC behind the speed

Personality: precise, measured, established, technical with a human sign-off — Stripe Sessions-like.
Message shape: mechanism ("here's why it gets to production") carried by a workflow, closing on a claim.
House look: none of the three is dark-with-warm-accent or cream-with-orange; A and B live in the
AI Builder's own pale-blue UI world, C in v7's parchment collage. The look is Kissflow's own.

A · The Pipeline       language: system-diagram + cursor-demo (gate chips light, real chat lines type, the BRD card rides the rail)   look: Graphik, the AI Builder's white cards on its pale-blue wash, soft UI shadows
Palette:  ground #EDF5FE 70 · surface #FFFFFF 20 · ink #0E1726 · muted #5B6678 · accent #1F80FF 4 · pass #4AA147 3 · draft #F5B66B 1 — from: aibuilder.kissflow.com pixels (c01, c10), Kissflow petals blue/green
Logline:  One continuous take along a seven-station rail — Requirements → Design → Verify → Sign-off → Build → Test → Version — while the agents' real chat log types beside it; at Verify the film bursts into fast cuts of the verifier catching and fixing, then stops dead on "Draft — awaiting sign-off". Ends: "You write the spec. The agents build exactly that." + logo.
Why it fits:  the message is a process; the rail makes "a gate after every step" literal and readable on mute.
Feel:  hybrid · 112 BPM · 2–3 events per bar, burst of 6 cuts at ~40 % · first 3 s: the rail snaps in with Requirements already ticking green and a BRD chip flying onto it
Risk:  can read as a slide diagram if the rail just sits there — the camera must travel along it.

B · Blueprint in Space   language: 3d-object / ui-space (the nine real Blueprint tabs as glass sheets in CSS 3D; one camera flies through the stack)   look: Graphik, real captures as lit sheets, blue rim light, depth falloff
Palette:  ground #E7F0FC→#C9DAF2 75 · sheets #FFFFFF 15 · ink #0E1726 · accent #1F80FF 5 (verifier scan plane) · pass #4AA147 2 — from: Blueprint UI captures c10–c18, Kissflow blue
Logline:  The camera starts inside the agents' chat, pulls back as nine documents peel off into a stack in space, a blue verifier plane sweeps down through every sheet stopping where it finds a flaw ("Possibly missing expressions"), then the stack locks, the top sheet reads "Draft — awaiting sign-off", and one click on Generate drops it into Kissflow.
Why it fits:  "the Blueprint is the bridge" becomes an object you can see from every side; depth gives the enterprise weight.
Feel:  continuous · 100 BPM · 1–2 events per bar, one burst as sheets fan out, a held stop before the scan · first 3 s: push through the BRD sheet already in frame
Risk:  UI text in 3D gets hard to read — every sheet must come to rest flat-on when it's the subject.

C · The Sign-off Desk   language: editorial-collage (real Blueprint pages printed on a desk, highlighter and pen draw on as narrated, v7's halftone cast reviewing them, a stamp lands)   look: Graphik on paper, v7 parchment, halftone cut-outs, petal-colour marks
Palette:  ground #E9E1D3 65 · paper #FFFEFB 25 · ink #14171C · highlighter #CF2C91 2 · pass stamp #4AA147 2 · link #1F80FF 2 — from: v7 film ground (sampled), Kissflow petals magenta/green/blue, BRD text
Logline:  Every SDLC artefact the agents produce lands on a reviewer's desk as a real page — the BRD, the data model, the permission matrix, the decision log — each marked up by the person who owns it; the verifier's fixes appear as red-pen corrections; the film ends on "Nothing gets built until you say so" and the Generate click.
Why it fits:  enterprise governance is people signing paper; keeps v7's cast and look as the thread.
Feel:  hybrid · 104 BPM · paper slides on the beat, a burst of 5 pages slapped down at ~45 % · first 3 s: the BRD page slides in, highlighter already striking "Draft — awaiting sign-off"
Risk:  "REVIEWED" stamp is a gesture, not a product button — must stay clearly human, never shown as an in-app approval.
