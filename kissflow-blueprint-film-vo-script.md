# Voiceover script

**V1** Vibe coding can build you an app in minutes.

**V2** But what you get is a prototype. And prototypes don’t belong in production.

**V3** Enterprise software demands SDLC governance: requirements, design, sign-off, testing. That process takes months.

**V4** Kissflow’s Agentic App Builder runs that entire process, in a fraction of the time.

**V5** It starts with your requirements. Agents read the BRD and map every role, rule, and journey.

**V6** Specialist agents design the data, the workflows, the permissions, and the screens. In parallel.

**V7** After every step, a verifier tries to break it. Lockouts. Dead ends. A bad formula. It fixes them — and checks again.

**V8** What you get isn't just an app. It's a Blueprint.

**V9** Nine documents your business, IT, and auditors can read — before a single screen exists.

**V10** Every decision is logged. Every open question is asked. Nothing gets built until you say so.

**V11** Then it builds — in your own Kissflow development environment, with your permissions — and tests every journey, for every role.

**V12** Every build is a version. Nothing reaches production on its own.

**V13** Rigorous, yet remarkably fast.

**V14** You write the spec. The agents build enterprise-grade software with governance built right in.

*End card text: matches V14, “You write the spec. The agents build enterprise-grade software with governance built right in.” · Kissflow logo \| Agentic App Builder*
