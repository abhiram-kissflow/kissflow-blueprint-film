## Music
World: **pulse minimalism** (Reich, *Music for 18 Musicians*) — the product is a process that ticks forward stage by
stage; interlocking marimba sixteenths are that pulse. **112 BPM**, A major → F♯ minor at the verify burst, back to A
for the Blueprint. Band: two marimbas, piano, strings pad, pizz bass; a soft kick + rim enter only for the burst,
drop out for the stop, return under the hero. Sonic logo: a 4-note marimba figure (A–C♯–E–A) on every station tick,
played whole on the end card. Ducks under Bria.

④ unfolds into the nine-sheet stack (hero) → stack lands flat as the BRD → its sign-off badge → Generate button →
click ripple runs along the rail into ⑤ → ⑥ ticks → Versions dropdown docks on ⑦ → pull back to the whole rail →
cut on the beat to the end card (the only uncarried cut, on purpose).
